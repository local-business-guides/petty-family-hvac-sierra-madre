What to prepare for installation

The exact work depends on the home and the equipment being installed. A contractor needs to review the existing system and understand the project before outlining the work. From there, you can talk through the scope, equipment options, and practical arrangements for the installation.

Here’s a useful homeowner checklist:
- Make a list of rooms that are often too warm or too cool.
- Note whether you need heating, air conditioning, or both.
- Gather information about the current equipment, if available.
- Clear a path to the equipment and discuss access to the work area.
- Ask what you should move or prepare before the scheduled work.

Avoid selecting equipment based only on appearance or assuming that a unit matching the old one is automatically the right fit. Don’t remove or disconnect existing equipment yourself. Share your questions and let a professional assess the setup before work begins.
